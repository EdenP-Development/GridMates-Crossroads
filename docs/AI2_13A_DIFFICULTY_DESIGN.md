# AI2-13A — Difficulty design contract

Three experimental levels, separate from the six personalities. Standard maps exactly to selected existing policy IDs. Relaxed candidate reduces emphasis on shortest remaining distance and raw target setback; Advanced candidate rewards visible encounter-free finish opportunities, diverse upcoming forks and denying an imminent finish. These are uncalibrated decision heuristics, not demonstrated strength ordering. Preserve every legal action, dice/event probabilities, movement/reward rules and human information boundaries. No future RNG, handicaps or deliberate illegal actions. Candidate scoring helpers only, not dispatched or persisted. For integration prefer distinct immutable policy IDs per personality/difficulty pair (Standard reuses current IDs); no schema migration needed, and older saves retain their IDs. Test all pairs with mirrored matches, compare per-personality strength and behavior, do not expose labels until evidence supports them. Classic and Traveler currently use legacy randomized route scoring; integration must explicitly design how to preserve their identity without mutating standard behavior. Difficulty may affect routes/targets first; travel and encounter tuning only if evidence requires. Physical QA and fairness remain outstanding.
