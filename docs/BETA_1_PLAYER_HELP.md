# Crossroads Beta 1 — player troubleshooting draft

For the public Android 8.0+ edition only. Do not publish until the signed candidate and official download/contact/privacy links are confirmed.

**Cannot install:** Check Android version and available storage. Download only the official public APK; extract ZIP first if applicable. Android may ask permission to install from the chosen trusted source. Keep Play Protect enabled. Unexpected warning or package conflict: stop and contact the publisher, do not uninstall or clear data to force installation.

**Update fails:** Confirm it is the public edition, not Crossroads QA, and a newer version. Preserve the old installation and export a backup. Contact publisher with version and error; do not downgrade.

**Progress missing:** QA and public editions have separate storage. Use a compatible original-rules backup exported from About & data. Import replaces local records and saved race after confirmation; back up the destination first. Experimental QA races may be rejected. Keep exported JSON private; it is not encrypted.

**No internet or multiplayer option:** Solo and same-device Pass & Play work offline. Nearby Wi-Fi and experimental modes are not part of public Beta 1.

**Game stuck or layout issue:** Record the course, active player, last action and device/Android version; reopen normally and note whether the saved race resumes. Do not clear storage. Send reproduction steps and optional screenshot through confirmed support channel. Never post private backup files publicly.

Publisher contact and permanent privacy URL pending. No guarantee of every device or recovery of deleted files.
