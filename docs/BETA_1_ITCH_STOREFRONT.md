GridMates: Crossroads is a cozy woodland board game where everyone shares the same destination, but no two journeys need to be alike. Take a shortcut, follow a safer path, or risk an unexpected encounter as you race toward the finish.

- Play solo against AI opponents.
- Gather 2–4 players for local Pass & Play.
- Explore three handcrafted woodland courses: Quick, Classic and Long.
- Discover helpful shelters, hazards and mischievous encounters.
- Explore the full board and revisit your journey journal.
- Save your progress and return whenever you're ready.

No accounts. No advertisements. No mandatory internet connection for gameplay. Just a board, some dice, and a journey to share.

About Beta 1: Crossroads is an independently developed game from Spudacus and part of the GridMates family. This early public beta focuses on the original board-racing experience. Additional gameplay modes and multiplayer features are being developed separately. Feedback is welcome.

Android 8.0 or newer (API 26); device compatibility subject to physical testing.
