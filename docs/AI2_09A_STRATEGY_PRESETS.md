# AI2-09A — Strategy presets and difficulty design

Separate personality/strategy from difficulty. Existing V1/R1/R2/R3/R4 are not a monotonic strength ladder: R2 was above equal-share baseline in both two/four-seat tournaments, R3/R4 tied in two-seat and differed in four-seat. Presets CLASSIC, TRAVELER, SCOUT, GUARDIAN, STRATEGIST resolve existing immutable policies for offline experimentation only. These names are descriptive provisional aliases, not proof of distinct balanced personalities. Guardian inherits R3's conditional protection, not universally cautious. Gambler requires a separately designed policy and calibration; do not rename existing IDs or equate risk-taking with difficulty. No new enum policy IDs, RNG, UI or LAN changes.

Proposed difficulty design: evaluate forgiving, standard and advanced decision policies with same dice/event probabilities, mirrored seats, course/player-count stratification and player experience tests. No hidden boosts or penalties. Do not label current presets as difficulty levels before evidence. Preserve resolved per-seat policies in existing checkpoint fields and rematches. Next: measure preset behavior on convergence candidate, then fairness experiment and calibrated difficulty candidates. Physical QA remains outstanding.
