# GridMates: Crossroads — Privacy policy

**Draft for Beta 1 — not yet effective.** Applies to the public Android edition published under Spudacus / Eden Practical, not internal QA builds. Confirm publisher identity, effective date and contact before publishing.

## Data on your device

Crossroads is an offline board game. It does not require an account. The public Beta 1 app does not send gameplay data to the developer or use advertising, analytics or cloud synchronization. Nearby network play is not available in this edition.

The game stores local preferences, wins, completed match counts, and a saved offline race (including the game seed, actions, player seats, and completion information) to support continued play and recovery. This information stays in the app's local storage unless you choose to export it.

## Backups and deletion

You can export a plain-text JSON backup using Android's document picker. You choose the destination and control who can access it. A third-party storage provider selected by you may handle the file under its own policy. Backups are not encrypted by Crossroads. Keep them in a private location.

Import requires confirmation and replaces existing local records and the saved race; incompatible or invalid files are rejected. Internal QA experimental matches may not be supported by the public app. Exported files are separate from the app's local storage.

Local information remains until you replace or delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Automatic Android app backup is disabled in the manifest; device transfer behavior can vary. Keep a manual backup before changing devices or uninstalling. Delete exported files separately when no longer needed.

## Permissions and external services

The public app does not request Internet, network-state, Wi-Fi-state or multicast permissions. Android provides a document picker for user-directed file access. The AndroidX app-specific receiver permission is used internally, not for network access or broad file access.

The download storefront and any voluntary support or feedback channels operate separately from the game and may process information under their own policies. Do not send backup files or personal information in public feedback.

## Children and changes

The app does not ask for personal information or offer public chat in this edition. This policy does not establish a child-directed audience designation or legal compliance certification. Any future online features will require updated disclosures before release.
